Why film location management translates to ops consulting

A film location manager doesn't just find pretty buildings. I negotiate contracts with property owners. I build logistical maps for crew parking, base camps, and equipment staging. I manage budgets that track daily expenses down to petty-cash receipts. I draft and execute legal agreements with indemnification clauses, insurance requirements, and security deposits. I coordinate with municipal governments for permits. I canvass neighbourhoods for resident approval.

Sound familiar? It's the exact same operational infrastructure every trades contractor, property manager, and service business needs — but almost none of them have it built properly.

The difference between a production that runs smoothly and one that hemorrhages money is always the same thing: systems. Documented processes. Automated handoffs. Clear accountability. That's what I build for your business.

What Throughline Operations does differently

Most "AI agencies" hand you a chatbot and disappear. I sit inside your operations, find the line running through every day — the work that repeats — and build operational intelligence around it. The missed calls, the invoices that slip through cracks, the scheduling chaos that burns your team's time.

Then I build you working systems. Not slide decks. Not strategy documents. Actual automations, documented and handed over so your team owns them — built to augment your people, never to replace them.

I focus on Hamilton and the GTA because I know this market and these pressures. And I price in fixed-scope sprints, so the math is simple: a sprint that saves your team hours every week pays for itself fast.
