01 / What we heard

This plan starts with your words, not our ideas.

  • Timesheets consume 4–8 hours every weekend — your highest-value time, spent on paperwork.
  • The inbox never empties. Real opportunities sit under routine noise.
  • Job details, driver info, and client history live in different places. Finding anything means remembering where it is.
  • Scheduling and driver outreach are manual, one call and one text at a time.
  • You have the ability to branch into traffic control here any time, and the international opportunities are real. AFD is growing whether the paperwork cooperates or not.
02 / What that costs today
Timesheets alone: 4–8 hrs/weekend = 200–400 hours per year. At even a modest value on your time, that's a five-figure annual leak — before counting missed inbox opportunities and hours lost to scattered information. The plan below is designed to hand those hours back.

04 / The growth roadmap — inside Option 4

Your operation, staffed up over time.

Each piece added when you're ready:

Accounting workflow, first

Receipts and invoices captured and categorized automatically as drafts — you or Maedot review and approve everything. You stay in control; the paperwork stops piling.

Asset research

You set the goal and criteria; it scans and reports on purchases worth your attention.

Client acquisition

Leads found, qualified, and queued for your review.

Social presence

LinkedIn and social drafts, on brand, approved by you before anything posts.

The dashboard and memory system are also the foundation for where AFD is headed internationally — one operational brain, wherever the work is.

05 / What this is not
  • Not a replacement for you, Maedot, or a single driver. This augments your team; it never replaces it.
  • Not software you have to learn. You talk to it like a dispatcher.
  • Not a data grab. Your systems are dedicated to AFD alone, handled PIPEDA-aligned, and nothing sends or spends without human approval.
  • Not a lock-in. Month-to-month care; your data is always yours.
