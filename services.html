How the sprint runs

Three focused weeks, mostly remote — built around your schedule, not disrupting it. You're hands-on for roughly three short calls; everything else happens on our side.

Week 1 — Kickoff & leak hunt

A working call to walk through a normal week — your tools, your team, your workflow, where the pain is. We map every manual touchpoint and surface your top leaks by cost.

Week 2 — Plan sign-off & build

You pick the priority; we build to your decision. The automation is built inside your existing tools — typically an n8n workflow wired to your email, sheets, and calendar — and tested against real scenarios from your business.

Week 3 — Live testing & handoff

We run it live with real data while you watch, adjust on the spot, and walk you through the playbook so you own it. You get a full written sprint report: what was built, how to run it, and what to automate next.

Residency vs. sovereignty — why it matters

Your data stays in Canada, governed by Canadian law.

Data residency is where your data physically sits. Data sovereignty is whose law governs it. Cloud-hosted-in-Toronto gives you residency — but not sovereignty. We can truthfully offer both only with an on-premises deployment.

100% Canadian data residency

Your AI models and corporate data are physically hosted on hardware located entirely within Canada.

True Canadian data sovereignty

Because the system operates on-premises, your data is governed exclusively by Canadian law — insulated from the extraterritorial reach of the U.S. CLOUD Act.

Closed-loop architecture

Eliminating third-party cloud processing drastically reduces your attack surface; your proprietary data never leaves your facility for model training.

Engineered for compliance

Localized infrastructure provides the auditable control to align with PIPEDA standards, simplify Law 25 transfer assessments, and meet Ontario Bill 194 mandates.
